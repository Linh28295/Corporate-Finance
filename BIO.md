# Bio — Nguyen Bui Ngoc Linh

Nguyen Bui Ngoc Linh is a strategy and transformation leader with 8 years of experience across life insurance, retail, and financial services in Vietnam.

Currently Senior Manager at Prudential Vietnam, she leads a multi-disciplinary team spanning strategic planning, financial governance, and change management — overseeing a USD 10M transformation portfolio and USD 50M budget remit, with USD 10M+ in delivered cost savings to date.

Her career has been built through progressive corporate strategy roles — from PNJ Group and AIA Vietnam to Prudential Vietnam — with consistent focus on translating executive ambition into operational reality. She holds a PROSCI change management certification, is a two-time recipient of Prudential's Alexandrite Award, and is currently completing an Executive MBA at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa (AACSB-accredited).
