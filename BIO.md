# Bio — Nguyen Bui Ngoc Linh

Nguyen Bui Ngoc Linh is a strategy and transformation leader with eight years of experience across life insurance, retail, and financial services in Vietnam.

Currently Senior Manager, Operations & Technology Strategy & Transformation at Prudential Vietnam, she leads a multi-disciplinary team spanning strategic planning, financial governance, and change management — driving a USD 10M annual transformation roadmap, USD 50M budget remit, and USD 10M+ in delivered cost savings to date through structured optimisation across vendor contracts, operating-model redesign, and non-value-adding activity elimination.

Her career has progressed through dedicated corporate strategy roles at three organisations: Assistant to the Chief Strategy Officer at PNJ Group (building business-planning and competitive-analysis foundations), Supervisor of Business Strategy at AIA Vietnam (advancing performance reporting and C-suite strategic decision support), and Manager of Corporate Strategy at Prudential Vietnam (owning enterprise-wide strategic planning before stepping into her current transformation-leadership role). She holds a PROSCI change management certification, is a two-time recipient of Prudential's Alexandrite Award (Health Strategy 2024, Claims BPR 2026), was selected for Prudential Vietnam Assurance's Talent Group in both 2025 and 2026, and is currently completing an Executive MBA at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa's Shidler College of Business (AACSB-accredited).
